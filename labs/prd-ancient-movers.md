## 2. The Two Movers (standing canon, condensed)

**PERSONAL MOVERS** are player-owned: a granted mount on the RideService
ledger, a platform woken by the player's own tablet, an owned glider, a
breather helmet. They wear honestly, repair FREE at stations, take Mender's
Kits into the field and bench modifiers under the Tide-Mender Trade
(`prd-monetization.md` sec 29). A personal mover never rivals; it rides.

**ANCIENT MOVERS** are the server's own: the ferries on their honesty law,
the dormant ruin movers, the islets' orb machines, and every ancient yet
unwritten that carries people, cargo or a route. An ancient belongs to no
player, never prices a personal SKU, and is never sold to one — its bill is
a CIVIC bill (settlement deeds, warden take, audited Concord sink rows).
No ancient ever appears in the trader's window, the catalog, or any SKU.

## 3. Scope: More Than Movers

The Mystery Gate (sec 6) governs EVERY server-unlocked thing, not only
vehicles: ancient movers, sealed gates, hidden abilities, dormant world
physics, anything deep that the world holds back. When a future PRD writes a
sealed door, a locked verb, or a physics secret, it is an ancient in this
document's sense and rides these laws.

## 4. The No-Technology Law

Synmergia has no concept of engines or technology. The ancient machinery is
DORMANT SYMBIOTIC WORK — geometric conduit forms overgrown by root, moss and
mycelium, already loose in the world before anyone claims them. There is no
fuel to buy, no engine to build, no tech tree anywhere.

- A MEND is binding work: re-binding root and wrapper, re-blessing the orb,
  re-lacing a conduit seam (the Tide-Mender Trade's museum work).
- A MODIFIER is verse work: knot-charms, tablet statutes, conduit arcanum
  laced through the mover's own dormant body — capability verbs only, never
  a combat rate, never a travel-speed ceiling.

## 5. The Lifecycle of an Ancient

An ancient moves through four states, tracked server-side, never client-
published as progress:

- **SLEEPING** — dormant. May be invisible (a shape under soil or tide) or
  standing in plain sight as ruin. Its keys accrue nothing here.
- **SIGHTED** — seen and known: a visible, inert great shape (the islets'
  orb machines at first landing, the ruin movers at their dig). The world
  may make hints about it (a monument's text, a dock's chart); it stays
  inert.
- **WOKEN** — the keys turned: it runs. The wake is an EVENT the world
  prints (a rise, a light, a route opening), authored by the ancient's own
  event family. Its civic bill begins (the standing bill: the Civic Bill
  below).
- **AT REST** — long disuse lowers it again: the road forgets what the road
  no longer serves, and the ancient re-sleeps SIGHTED, its route closed,
  the same keys able to wake it again later. An ancient is never deleted;
  dormancy is its natural home.

### The Civic Bill and the Steward's Pledge (spitball #21, 2026-11-05)

An ancient's upcost is civic and it is LEDGERED: every woken ancient
accrues STEWARDSHIP through the audited sink rows (a TAKE-family book
beside the roll board) — settlement deeds pay most of it, the warden
take covers roads and crossings. An ancient whose stewardship runs dry
takes the AT REST drift on its own schedule: the road forgets what the
road no longer serves, the same keys wake it again. The bill is never a
punishment and never a countdown printed at players (no wiki law).

THE STEWARD'S PLEDGE: a settlement formally ADOPTS one ancient —
rank-gated and vault-payable on the heraldry shape, mix-tracked by the
purse law (an earned crew holds the identical pledge wholly on earned
sigils). The pledging settlement's deed plate reads the adoption as
file truth ("the pans keep the Gullwing" — authored template, no free
text). The pledge buys nothing but SEQUENCE: no ceiling, no rank, no
stat, no cheaper bill — and NO DISCOUNT ON ANY TOLL: stewardship is its
own ledger, never a pass on the permits, deeds and gates the world
already prices (tolls orthogonal; what is bought is the ancient's
continued care, never relief on anything else). And when care is
relief's cousin — the owner's framing is "a great opt on way to ease
the PAIN of being taxed" — the ease is ADOPTION, not exemption: paying
the ancients' bill is joining the civilization instead of merely
inhabiting it.

## 6. The Mystery Gate

Every ancient (and every thing in sec 3's scope) holds its wake-key behind
the world, never before the store. The five WAKE-KEYS, each an open lane;
an ancient may hold one or many:

1. **THE PUZZLE** — a server puzzle wakes it: riddles spread across the
   world's own texts, monuments and seasons, solved by players, never by
   docs the team publishes. Design posture: the riddle is written so the
   world ITSELF is the manual — no data appendix, no guide book, no
   achievement named after the solution.
2. **THE TRAM EFFORT** — the effortful road wakes it: trips walked, hauls
   made, crowds carried. A route's honest use is itself the key — the road
   wakes what the road serves. Effort accrues by real, repeated,
   sincere use, never by a purchase or a checkbox.
3. **THE OBSERVATION** — the patient watcher wakes it: things placed where
   nobody looks (a moon's shadow at a bearing, a light under an islet's
   root), reading and reporting rewarded. The world's watchers are an
   institution the game builds on, not a chore the game sells.
4. **THE INTERACTION** — the ritual hand wakes it: gestures, offerings,
   tablet statutes touched in the right order at the right ground. The
   seed's tablet-waking verb for the dormant trio lives HERE as a first
   verse of this key.
5. **THE PURE LUCK** — nobody wakes it; it wakes: a spawn, a season, or a
   roll turns its own key, and whoever stood there was the key. The
   spiritbind spirit — luck as the rarest letter in any economy.

Multi-key ancients are legal and encouraged: a mover whose door took BOTH
observation and the tram effort is a mystery that lasted. A multi-key
surface prints NO progress anywhere — the ledger counts, the players do the
mystifying.

## 7. The Sealed Keys

Some wake conditions are written ONLY server-side — absent from design
docs AND code PRs by design, sometimes beyond any one dev's knowledge by
design ("some even the devs don't know").

- **Sealed keys are MINTED, not authored**: where a key is sealed, the
  world derives it at boot from the shard's own seed (a one-way
  derivation over the ancient's id), so no human ever typed the answer and
  no human can read the answer back — neither from source, logs, or this
  doc. The keys' ledger lives server-side (proposed: the same keyed
  account-ledger family, sealed reads only), never client-visible, never
  dumped.
- **The discovery posture:** the team discovers alongside the players.
  Docs may speak of an ancient's FAMILY and its laws (always); docs never
  print a sealed key (never — not to heighten it, not "for the record",
  not even redacted). A sealed key is not a secret; it is a non-record.
- **The Oracles (paid, and still lawful — prd-monetization.md sec 31).**
  When a sealed door's wake-key includes a luck leg, two lanes sell the
  WIDER SHOT and nothing else: the WISDOM CALL (delegated Rust/Julia
  sidecar batches — attested extra dice, priced to cover their own
  compute) and the MEDITATION CEREMONY (the Sigil rite's bought ceremony
  days on the Lucky pot's shape). Both buy dice-COUNT, never a better
  die; no purchase opens the door, prints a hint, or touches the
  riddle/effort/watching/interaction keys.
- **Falsifiable only by the world:** when a sealed mystery is suspected,
  the community's evidence is the same evidence the team has: what the
  world did. No admin panel prints the answer, and no support tool
  confirms a guess.

## 9. The Laws

1. **Nothing purchasable opens a wake-key.** Kits, modifiers, seats,
   blessings, the ankh's rite — none of the Concord's Table's twenty-two
   priced rows wakes the unwoken by purchase. The Oracles (sec 31 of the
   monetization doc) sell only the WIDER SHOT — more dice in a week —
   and every account's own week rolls regardless. The Table prices what
   exists; the Gate wakes on dice, and a wallet buys dice, never the
   draw.
2. **The ancients are never a currency, a competition, or a leaderboard.**
   A wake is an event, not a rank; the first waker earns the story, never
   a per-human stat. The story is REAL CREDIT, law-dressed by the wake
   event (prd-monetization.md sec 32): the wake record names the first
   waker FOREVER — service-record credit, never custom text — and mints
   a suffix deed by construction, purchasable at nothing.
3. **No ancient furnishes a combat stat** to any who wake, ride or hold
   it — an ancient mover is a mover of PEOPLE, never a weapon platform.
4. **No wiki in-game.** No progress bar, no checklist, no achievement
   named for a wake-key, no UI panel that says how close a key is. The
   world's own texts and hints are the only manual the ancients get.
5. **No free text ever** — every ward's print is authored from server
   truth, the standing tradeoff.
6. **Every world PRD speaking of vehicles, gates or deep things crosslinks
   this document** and makes the two-movers distinction before it prices
   anything.
7. **Mystery is the momentum.** The rate that is rendered/authored is not
   a sprint number: an ancient's sleep is content in every state, and the
   turning-of-keys is the game's own cathedral — slow, communal, and the
   one place no purchase ever reaches.

## 10. The Investor Materials

For `prd-monetization.md` sec 28/30 readers: the Mystery Gate is the
UNPRICED engagement engine the ledger cannot dress. The Concord's Table
twenty rows are priced and audited; the ancients' waking is the engagement
curve that arrives free at the site of the world's own secrets —
discovery-era retention (community legends, whisper-ledger rumors,
the events that wake) with no SKU attached and no P2W surface anywhere.
The investor clause already crosslinks: "a Mystery Gate that prices
nothing... and no purchase ever opens a gate."

The owner named the wake event's psy for the investors, verbatim: "a
permanent ledger, stokes the ego, easy spend, hair trigger" — the wake
record names the first waker forever (a permanent ledger), the suffix
mints by the record itself (the ego stoked), and the celebration permit
sells at the emotional peak (an easy spend on a hair trigger). The
Civic Bill is the keeper line: ancients bill civically and the
Steward's Pledge turns their upkeep into an owned identity a settlement
pays to keep — care is the bill, mystery follows care.
