# Crafting Stations (Gemini brief pack)

> **Goal:** the crafting/trade station set -- the machines players
> stand at. The blacksmith interior (sec. 8.4) established the
> grammar (dormant machine + roots + one Conduit console); this pack
> turns it into a repeatable station family.
>
> **Canon anchors:** BlacksmithService (furnace station rendered),
> the lit workbench Conduit (dwelling, sec. 8.10), Iron Ore
> (`res_iron_ore.tres`) as the material face. Station color law: the
> station WAKES when used -- teal idle, gold active -- which doubles
> as in-world status feedback.

**Station law for this pack:** each station is a half-buried machine
organ repurposed by crafters -- roots and moss on the housing, a
small teal Conduit panel bolted on, NO violet or magenta anywhere.
Where shown in use, the working face glows GOLD (active) and sparks
are warm.
