# Mounts & Companions (Gemini brief pack)

> **Goal:** rideable constructs and small companion fauna. There is
> no mount/pet data in `game/data/` yet, so this pack is the design
> seed: everything obeys the unpowered-world pitch (dormant symbiotic
> tech woken by tablets -- NO engines, NO mechanical cars, NO robots
> with motors) and the fauna canon (`<Substance>-<Animal>`, animal
> silhouette first, tech second).
>
> **Canon anchors:** the fauna set (Data-Owl, Spore-Spider,
> Root-Crab, Lichen-Lizard, Mycelial-Mantid, Luminous-Lemur) and the
> hostility key -- mounts and companions are NEVER magenta.

---

## 0. STYLE LOCK (paste the full paragraph into EVERY prompt -- never a placeholder token)

```
Stylized cyber-organic fantasy concept art, inked outlines with
cel-shaded flats and painted texture. A world of ancient dormant
symbiotic technology overgrown by nature: geometric circuit forms
wrapped in roots, moss, and mycelium. Desaturated grey-green base
palette (#c0c0c0 stone, #404040 shadow, #202020 dark) with three
semantic accent colors ONLY: pale teal/cyan (#80c0c0) for dormant or
waking technology, warm gold (#c0a060) for active converged
technology, violet (#8060c0) exclusively for mycelium and arcane
magic. No photorealism, no low-poly look, no neon cyberpunk
aesthetic. Clean white-grey presentation background, 16:9 sheet with
a thin margin, hand-lettered style labels under each figure (short
labels only, no paragraphs of text). Render ONLY the exact label
words listed at the end -- no filenames, no other text.
```

**Hard rule for this pack:** no magenta anywhere (tame creatures);
violet only on the Spore-Spider hatchling's cap (mycelium). Saddle
gear is leather, rope, root-woven straps, and small teal wake-clasps
-- the tablet wakes the mount, so the tack has a tablet cradle.
