# Species & Class Concept Sheets (Gemini brief pack)

> **Goal:** one character sheet per playable species + one trio hero
> sheet. This closes the biggest art gap (the current set has zero
> character designs) and gives character-select real art to sit on.
>
> **Canon anchors:** `game/data/species/species_{human,akkan,synthetic}.tres`
> and `game/data/classes/class_*.tres`. The stat text below is
> authoritative -- where Gemini drifts, the .tres files win.

---

## 0. STYLE LOCK (paste this at the top of EVERY prompt below)

```
Stylized cyber-organic fantasy concept art, inked outlines with
cel-shaded flats and painted texture. A world of ancient dormant
symbiotic technology overgrown by nature: geometric circuit forms
wrapped in roots, moss, and mycelium. Desaturated grey-green base
palette (#c0c0c0 stone, #404040 shadow, #202020 dark) with three
semantic accent colors ONLY: pale teal/cyan (#80c0c0) for dormant or
waking technology, warm gold (#c0a060) for active converged
technology, violet (#8060c0) exclusively for mycelium and arcane
magic. No photorealism, no low-poly look, no neon cyberpunk city
aesthetic. Clean white-grey presentation background, 16:9 sheet with
a thin margin, hand-lettered style labels under each figure (short
labels only, no paragraphs of text).

IMPORTANT TEXT RULE: render ONLY the exact label words listed at the
end of each prompt. Do NOT render filenames, .tres extensions, hex
codes, or any other text -- anything else becomes gibberish or drifts
(misspelled wordmarks like "MIY WORDMARK"). Decorative glyph scribbles
are fine; readable wrong words are not.
```

## 1. The Three Species (one sheet, front-facing lineup)

**Feel:** three answers to the same world. Humans adapt and repair;
Akkans remember war; Synthetics are the world's own tech that woke up.

**Prompt:**

```
[STYLE LOCK]

Character lineup sheet titled "SYNMERGIA: THE THREE SPECIES", four
front-facing full-body figures on a white-grey background, evenly
spaced, same scale, soft ground shadows:

1. HUMAN EXPLORER (center-left): lean adventurer in layered
   field-gear -- hooded grey-green travel cloak over fitted leather
   and canvas armor, brass buckles, one shoulder pauldron of polished
   salvage-plate. Carries a rectangular glowing tablet on a hip strap
   (pale teal glow). Dusty boots, rope coil, small pouch of circuit
   fragments. Confident scavenger posture.

2. AKKAN WARRIOR (right): tall, broad, heavy-boned humanoid with
   grey-violet skin (#a0a0c0 family), deep-set ember-orange eyes,
   dark braided hair. Ancient angular war-plate in dark bronze and
   iron with worn gold trim and carved clan sigils, a massive
   two-handed slab-sword resting point-down, fur-lined mantle.
   Monumental, patient menace.

3. SYNTHETIC CONDUIT (far right): elegant humanoid machine, matte
   pale-ceramic and dark-alloy plating, exposed fine circuit tracery
   glowing faint teal across the chest and arms, a smooth featureless
   faceplate with two soft light "eyes", thin coiled cable-tail from
   the lower back. Hums with restrained power -- dormant, not dead.

4. Small head-study row along the bottom edge: two alternate heads
   per species (hair/plate variants), no labels on this row.

Labels: "HUMAN", "AKKAN", "SYNTHETIC" only -- keep text minimal and
correctly spelled. Do not render any other text.
```

> **Status:** rendered + canonized 2026-09-25. The Human species
> sheet from this brief is the Human reference (labs/art-direction.md
> sec. 8.6); note the bottom anchor row rendered with .tres-filename
> labels -- the no-filenames text rule above is the fix for reruns.

## 2. Human Classes (one sheet, action poses)

**Canon classes:** Warrior, Guardian, Marksman, Sorcerer, Cleric,
Temporalist. The Temporalist is the flavor flagship -- time-weaver.

**Prompt:**

```
[STYLE LOCK]

Class sheet titled "SYNMERGIA: HUMAN CLASSES", six full-body figures
in two rows of three, dynamic but readable poses, consistent scale:

Row 1:
- WARRIOR: battered half-plate over crimson-tabard leather, notched
  longsword and round repair-shield (shield face is a dormant circuit
  mandala, faint teal). Mid-swing pose.
- GUARDIAN: tower-shield bracer on one arm, heavy salvage-plate with
  teal-lit convergence studs, mace with a hexagonal head. Rooted
  defensive stance, shield forward.
- MARKSMAN: scavenged longrifle whose stock is grown through with
  living root-wood, scope patched from lens salvage, folded cloak in
  olive-khaki. Kneeling, aiming at off-sheet target.

Row 2:
- SORCERER: long grey coat with circuit-embroidered hems (violet
  thread ONLY -- arcane), one gauntlet of fossilized mycelium-plate
  leaking soft violet motes, staff crowned with a crystal grown
  around a metal core. Casting stance, violet sigil ring at feet.
- CLERIC: white-and-brass field vestments over chain, halo-arch
  relic frame floating behind the head (gold, active convergence
  tech), mace-and-tome. Healing pose, gold light settling on a
  wound.
- TEMPORALIST (hero of the sheet): sand-beige longcoat with a
  fractured clock-motif pauldron, twin glowing tablets orbiting one
  raised hand (teal), a slow ring of floating seconds-glyphs -- tiny
  geometric ticks, NOT readable numerals. Calm face, closed eyes,
  absolute control. Render slightly larger than the other five.

Labels: class names only, correctly spelled. No other text.
```

> **Status:** rendered + canonized 2026-09-25 as the class-select
> reference set (all 18 classes). Permuted-class drift and single-
> figure hero shots remain open (labs/art-direction.md sec. 6).

## 3. Akkan Classes (one sheet)

**Canon classes:** Bulwark, Stalker, Runeblade (Runesmith), Seer,
Shaman, Attacker. Martial + sigil culture from the dark highlands.

**Prompt:**

```
[STYLE LOCK]

Class sheet titled "SYNMERGIA: AKKAN CLASSES", six figures, two rows
of three. All Akkan: tall heavy-boned humanoids, grey-violet skin,
ember-orange eyes, dark braids, angular dark-bronze war-plate with
worn gold trim and carved sigils. Same species face across all six --
only armor silhouettes change:

Row 1:
- BULWARK: near-immobile siege plate, tower shield fused with a
  dormant door-seal of the Convergence (teal circuit mandala slowly
  waking gold at the center), warhammer. Nothing gets past.
- STALKER: light layered plate over shadow-cloth, twin curved
  daggers with cable-wrapped hilts, face half-shadowed by a carved
  half-mask. Crouched on a broken conduit pipe.
- RUNEBLADE: slab-sword whose blade is an inscribed rune-tablet --
  carved sigils glow ember-orange (Akkan sigil color, distinct from
  tech teal), detonation sigils dangling from the belt. Mid-draw.

Row 2:
- SEER: skull-plate helm with an ember third-eye slit, bone-and-bronze
  oracle staff, storm-grey mantle. Reading a cluster of floating
  ember sigils like embers off a fire.
- SHAMAN: heavy furs and totem-plate, bundle of spirit-bound talismans
  (each a small carved metal tag on braided cable), drum of wound
  steel-hide. Summoning stance, ember spirit-wisps circling.
- ATTACKER: twin short-axes, minimal fast plate with exposed
  sigil-scarred skin beneath, war-kilt of cable and bronze. Full
  sprint charge pose.

Labels: class names only, correctly spelled. No other text.
```

> **Status:** rendered + canonized 2026-09-25. Ember-orange stays
> Akkan-only in the rendered set (sigils, eyes) -- the palette audit
> confirms no teal/gold/violet bleed onto Akkan sigils.

## 4. Synthetic Classes (one sheet)

**Canon classes:** Conduit, Exploiter, Overclocker, Psion, Tracker,
Warden. The world's own machinery that woke up and chose sides.

**Prompt:**

```
[STYLE LOCK]

Class sheet titled "SYNMERGIA: SYNTHETIC CLASSES", six figures, two
rows of three. All Synthetics: humanoid machines of matte
pale-ceramic and dark-alloy plating, smooth featureless faceplates
with soft light eyes, visible fine circuit tracery, thin cable-tails.
Silhouettes must differ strongly; face design stays consistent:

Row 1:
- CONDUIT (the icon): balanced herald-plate, chest core = the Agent
  Core emblem (the star-core logo) glowing soft teal, energy arcs
  between raised fingertips. The "default" synthetic.
- EXPLOITER: asymmetric intrusion rig, one oversized gauntlet of
  hooked salvage-tools and probe spines, hacked teal-to-gold circuit
  patches (stolen active power). Crouched mid-breach on a dormant
  conduit.
- OVERCLOCKER: vented berserk frame, heat-sink fins flared from the
  back and shoulders glowing gold-hot, steam wisps, piston-limbs.
  Power-surge pose, ground cracking underfoot.

Row 2:
- PSION: smooth sealed body, no visible tools -- instead a violet
  mycelium-fiber mantle grown INTO the shoulder plating, violet
  telekinetic shards orbiting an open palm (violet ONLY, arcane).
  Floating a hand-span off the ground.
- TRACKER: needle-thin hunter frame, sensor-mast crest on the head,
  cloak of woven cable, one arm a folding sensor array (teal
  sweep-line). Mid-leap between tree-roots.
- WARDEN: heavy guardian frame with a riot barrier of hard-light
  hexagons (teal), capture pincer free hand, broad stable stance.
  A small Luminous-Lemur perches on the shoulder (canon fauna cameo).

Labels: class names only, correctly spelled. No other text.
```

> **Status:** rendered + canonized 2026-09-25. Synthetic duel look
> (lean ceramic frame, cable-tail, teal arcs) is pinned by the Akkan
> vs Synthetic duel key art (labs/art-direction.md sec. 8.5).

Single hero shot titled "SYNMERGIA", 16:9 key art: the three species

## Rendering checklist (for after generation)

- [ ] Species faces consistent within each sheet; silhouettes distinct
- [ ] Accent discipline: ember-orange ONLY on Akkan sigils; violet
      ONLY on Sorcerer/Psion/mycelium; gold only on active/warden/
      cleric elements -- flag any drift
- [ ] No paragraph text baked in; labels short and spelled right
- [ ] Same ink/outline weight as the world concept set
- [ ] Drop in Drive, attach favorites for critique; canonized names
      go into `game/data/classes/*.tres` descriptions only after sign-off
