# Faction Banners (Gemini brief pack)

> **Goal:** standards, seals, and faction marks for the three joinable
> species factions plus the Neutral mark -- for banner poles in
> territories, guild-hall chrome, nameplate accents, and the faction
> select screen.
>
> **Canon anchors:** `game/data/factions/faction_{akkan,human,synthetic,neutral}.tres`
> -- display names "Akkan Horde" (dark_highlands), "Human Alliance"
> (starter_plains), "Synthetic Collective" (nexus_central), "Neutral".

---

## 0. STYLE LOCK (paste the full paragraph into EVERY prompt -- never a placeholder token)

```
Stylized cyber-organic fantasy concept art, inked outlines with
cel-shaded flats and painted texture. A world of ancient dormant
symbiotic technology overgrown by nature: geometric circuit forms
wrapped in roots, moss, and mycelium. Desaturated grey-green base
palette (#c0c0c0 stone, #404040 shadow, #202020 dark) with three
semantic accent colors ONLY: pale teal/cyan (#80c0c0) for dormant or
waking technology, warm gold (#c0a060) for active converged
technology, violet (#8060c0) exclusively for mycelium and arcane
magic. No photorealism, no low-poly look, no neon cyberpunk
aesthetic. Clean white-grey presentation background, 16:9 sheet with
a thin margin, hand-lettered style labels (short labels only).
Render ONLY the exact label words given -- no filenames, no other
text.
```

**Faction color law (use in every prompt):** Akkan Horde carries
ember-orange sigils + worn gold on dark bronze (never teal/violet);
Human Alliance carries teal circuit embroidery on grey-green salvage
cloth; Synthetic Collective carries teal tracery on pale ceramic +
dark alloy. Gold = rank tassels/finials for all three. Violet and
magenta are FORBIDDEN on all faction marks.

## 1. The Three Standards (one sheet)

**Prompt:**

```
[STYLE LOCK -- expanded]

Faction sheet titled "SYNMERGIA: STANDARDS", three tall banners on
wood-and-alloy poles in a row against a white-grey background, each
hanging from a crossbar, same scale, gentle cloth sag, ground
shadows:

1. AKKAN HORDE: a broad vertical banner of dark bronze-woven cloth
   with worn gold trim and black fur fringe; its mark is a carved
   slab-sword over a mountain cairn, the sigil lines burning
   ember-orange (Akkan sigil color only). Iron finial: a small
   cairn-stone stack.

2. HUMAN ALLIANCE: a patchwork banner of grey-green salvage cloth
   stitched with brass buckles and repair seams; its mark is the
   Agent Core star quartered with a small tablet glyph, embroidered
   in pale teal circuit thread (dormant tech = the human edge).
   Finial: a brass compass-ring.

3. SYNTHETIC COLLECTIVE: a precision-cut vertical standard of pale
   ceramic panels on a dark alloy frame, seams lit with thin teal
   tracery; its mark is the Agent Core star alone, centered, ringed
   by a calm teal mandala (the star is their origin mark). Finial:
   a knotted cable tassel.

Labels: "SYNMERGIA: STANDARDS", "AKKAN HORDE", "HUMAN ALLIANCE",
"SYNTHETIC COLLECTIVE" only. No other text.
```
